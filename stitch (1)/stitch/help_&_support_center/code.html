Common Questions

sell How do I list a book for sale? expand_more
To list a book, tap the '+' icon in the bottom navigation bar. Upload photos, enter the title, ISBN, and condition, then set your price.
verified_user Is payment secure? expand_more
Yes, all payments are held in escrow until the buyer confirms receipt of the item. We use industry-standard encryption for all transactions.
school How do I verify my student ID? expand_more
Go to your profile settings and tap "Verify Student Status". Upload a clear photo of your current student ID card or use your .edu email address.
location_on Safe meeting spots on campus? expand_more
We recommend meeting in public, well-lit areas such as the Student Union, Library lobby, or designated campus safe trading zones.
